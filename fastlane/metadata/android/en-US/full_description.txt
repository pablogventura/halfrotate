HalfRotate keeps Android auto-rotation enabled but blocks upside-down (180°) and reverse landscape (270°). Only portrait (0°) and landscape (90°) are allowed.

<b>Features</b>
• No root required
• Respects system auto-rotate when you turn it off
• Quick Settings tile
• Restores after reboot
• No ads, no tracking
• Open source (GPL-3.0-or-later)

<b>Permissions</b>
• Modify system settings — rotation only
• Foreground service — while filter is active, with notification

<b>Note:</b> A brief screen flash may occur when correcting 270° to 90°. This is a system limitation without root.
