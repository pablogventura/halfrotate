HalfRotate filters Android system auto-rotation to the orientations you choose: portrait + landscape (default), portrait only, landscape only, or all except upside down.

While the filter is active, system auto-rotate is locked and HalfRotate rotates directly between allowed orientations — no flash through blocked angles.

<b>Features</b>
• Four rotation presets
• Optional force auto-rotation when system auto-rotate is off
• Anti-flicker proactive rotation engine
• No root required
• Quick Settings tile
• Restores after reboot
• No ads, no tracking
• Open source (GPL-3.0-or-later)

<b>Permissions</b>
• Modify system settings — rotation only
• Foreground service — while filter is active, with notification

<b>Note:</b> Some apps force their own orientation and may ignore system rotation.
